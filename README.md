# The Data Science Labor Market in America
### Mapping employment, wages, and occupational concentration across the United States.
This project explores the U.S. data science labor market using five years of employment and wage data (2021–2025) from the Bureau of Labor Statistics' Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics program. The interactive dashboard examines geographic differences in employment concentration, wages, and workforce trends across five analytical job families: Data Science, AI/ML Engineering, Statistics, Business Analytics, and Computer Science/Data Engineering. Built with Python, Streamlit, and Plotly, it combines interactive maps, ranked comparisons, scatterplots, a beeswarm visualization, and national employment and wage time series. Users can filter by year and occupational family to compare labor market patterns across states and explore how these fields have changed over time. One notable finding is the substantial growth in Data Science employment relative to the other job families between 2021 and 2025.
